## Probability Theory
**Definition of probability theory**
Probability theory is a method that helps us to analyze the chance of events in a logical soundly matter.
$$\leq P(Event) \leq 1$$
## Important Probability theory formulas
- ${} P(A^c)=1-p(A) {}$

- ${} P(A \cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B) {}$

- ${} P(A|B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)} {}$

- ${} P(B|A)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)} {}$
***If The two events are independent :-***
- ${} P(A \mid B)=P(A) {}$
- ${} P(B \mid A)=P(B) {}$
- ${} P(A \cap B)=P(A) \times P(B) {}$

**Definition Of entropy**
the measurement of degree of randomness or in other words, it is the increase in the disorganization within a system.
The value of entropy can be Positive, Negative or Infinite.
$$H(X)=-\sum_{j=1}^k P(X)\log(P(X))$$
## Mutual information
It is a measure of the amount of randomness shared between two random variables X and Y. It is defined as the difference between the entropy of X and the conditional entropy of X given Y, or the difference between the entropy of Y and the conditional entropy of Y given X.
$$ I(X;Y)=H(X)-H(X \mid Y)$$
$$I(X;Y)=H(Y)-H(Y \mid X)$$

***If the two events are independent on each other***
${} I(X;Y)=0 {}$
${} I(X;Y)<H(A),I(X;Y)<H(B) {}$

2. **Suppose 5 men out of 100 men and 10 women out of 250 women are color blind, then find the total probability of color blind people. (Assume that both men and women are in equal numbers.)** 
**<p style="text-align:center;">Solution</p>**
$$
\begin{align*}
P(\text{Color\,Blind}) &= P(\text{Color\,Blind} \mid \text{Man}) \cdot P(\text{Man}) + P(\text{Color\,Blind}  \mid \text{Woman}) \cdot P(\text{Woman}) \\
&= \left(\frac{5}{100}\right) \cdot \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) + \left(\frac{10}{250}\right) \cdot \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) \\
&= 0.045
\end{align*}
$$

4. **For three events A, B, and C, we know that A and C are independent and B and C are independent and A and B are disjoint P(A∪C)=2/3,P(B∪C)=3/4,P(A∪B∪C)=11/12.  Find : the probability P(A), P(B) , and P(C).**
**<p style="text-align:center;">Solution</p>**
Given the following equations:

$$P(A \cup C) = a + c - ac = \frac{2}{3} \tag{1}$$
$$P(B \cup C) = b + c - bc = \frac{3}{4} \tag{2}$$
$${} P(A \cup B \cup C) = a + b + c - ac - bc = \frac{11}{12} \tag{3}$$

Add equations (1) and (2):
$$
\begin{align*}
&(a + c - ac) + (b + c - bc) = \frac{2}{3} + \frac{3}{4} \\
&a + b + 2c - ac - bc = \frac{8}{12} + \frac{9}{12} = \frac{17}{12} \tag{4}
\end{align*}
$$
Now subtract equation (3) from equation (4):
$$
\begin{align*}
&(a + b + 2c - ac - bc) - (a + b + c - ac - bc) = \frac{17}{12} - \frac{11}{12} \\
&c = \frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2}
\end{align*}
$$
