 1. **What’s the time period of the Medieval music?**
	 - 500-1500 A.D.
 2. What is Gregorian chant?
	 - It was developed in 9th & 10th centuries it was what is known as plain chant it was named after St Gregory the great it was seen unaccompanied monophonic with single melody of lines. 
 3. **Why was most of the music anonymous, in the Medieval ?**
	 - The church had monopoly on creation of music and pieces were passed down through  word from mouth. Music was considered an act of devotion to the church and from this we don't know many of composes from this period.
 4. **Why Hildegard was the most famous music in the Medieval?**
	 - She was a German Abbess and herbalist we luckily know about her through her writings of alternative medicine. She composed about 69 pieces of music and traveled Germany preaching for reform. 
 5. **How was music developed through the Medieval period?**
	 - Around 1000 A.D. we saw development of polyphony which means more than one note played simultaneously including harmony, counterparts and chords.
 6. **What’s the similarity between medieval and modern music?**
	 - Some musical instruments which we can say were the ancestors of most of our musical instruments now day like Gittern which was played same as modern day guitar, Duclima which is made of strings and hit by small hammer this kind of has the same idea of modern day  xylophone, Trumpets which is still played today, Percussion instrument which generates sound by hitting them like drums nowadays, We also can see similarity in sound signature as they used 3,4 signature and now we most of time use 4,4 which is not much different as we know it had inspiration from christian trinity belief back then. Also there was rhythm modes which we also have in modern era. The use of monophonic music too with more than one melody of sound which is common in modern era
 7. **Did the church have a complete control over the music in this period ? Explain**
	 - Yes they had complete control as most of the music been defined by the church they also used the 3,4 sound signature which was an imitation for the trinity. The church and monks kept their music safe from decay by being the ones responsible for writing the music.
 8. **What are the main instruments in the Medieval? How they are similar to the ones in the modern era?**
	 - Fiddle, Gittern which is ancestor to the modern day guitar and kind of similar to it, Dulcimer which is played by striking strings with small hammers kind of like  modern day xylophone, Trumpets which is still used today in modern era and also there was percussion which is like drums which generate sound from hitting it.
