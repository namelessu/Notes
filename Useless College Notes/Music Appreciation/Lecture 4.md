1. **What is the main difference between acoustic treatment and soundproofing?**
	- Acoustic treatment is about reducing the sound reflections in room to improve clarity, Their materials are light to absorb mid to high frequencies.
	- Sound proofing: Is about blocking sound from entering or leaving the space, Their materials are denser and heavier to be able to block sounds.

5. **What are the three main types of acoustic treatments? What’s the use of each one of them?**

6. **Where are bass traps usually placed, and why?**
	- The bass traps usually put in the corners of the room because they absorb low frequencies.
7. **Where are absorption panels usually placed, and why?**
	- Absorption panels are usually placed on the walls to absorb mid to high frequencies and reduce echoes and reflections.
