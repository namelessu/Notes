1. **What is the definition of professional ethics?**
	>The professional ethics also known as "The Code Of Ethics" is a documentation of ethical and moral principles related to the field of profession that guides the individuals behavior according to their field of work.

2. **How do personal values differ from professional ethics?**
	>The personal value comes from each individual experience. But the professional ethics is standardized and documented to provide how each person should behave in accordance to his own profession. 
3. **What is a moral dilemma?**
	>The moral dilemma is when there are two obligations that come into conflict and in this case we try our best to prioritize and rank the most ethical and moral solutions after studying them well.
