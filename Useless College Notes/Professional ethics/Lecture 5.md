## Engineering Ethics
Engineering Ethics represent the attempts of professional engineers to define proper course of action in their dealings with each other, with their clients and employees, and the general public.
## Comparison between engineer and any other normal person.
Doctors and lawyers affect one person at a time, while engineers may affect hundreds if not thousands of people at the same time. So, engineering ethics are very important because they affect most of our everyday life.

## Code of ethics
A written set of guidelines issued by an organization to its workers and management to help them conduct their actions in accordance with its primary values and ethical standards.
