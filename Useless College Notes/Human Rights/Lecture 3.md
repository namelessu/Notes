## International Human Rights laws
- human rights law ensure that no one is discriminated or abused and has his own individual freedom.
- The human right law are applied at both international, regional and national levels.
- The UN(United nations) plays a very important role in defining the global human rights standards. The laws can be enforced by either through treaties, courts or monitoring bodies.
- Engineers must understand well this laws to ensure the most ethical practices in their own work.
###### **ICCPR(International convent of civil and political rights)**
- It was adopted in 1966 and enforced in 1976.
- Protects rights such as freedom of speech, right to fair trial and freedom from torture.
- Includes provisions on political participation and non-discrimination.
- Article 19: Guarantee freedom of expression and access to information.
- In 2016 United nations human rights committee condemned china restriction on internet and called for greater freedom, Although china didn't change it is own policies the ICCPR has been used by activists to pressure the government to respect digital right.
- Engineers shall ensure that they don't contribute to censorship technologies that violate human rights.
- Project dragonfly censored search engine was scrapped after global protests.
###### **ICESR(Internation Convenant on Econimic, Social and cultural rights)**
- Adopted 1966 and enforced 1976
- It protect rights related to work, education and standard of living.
